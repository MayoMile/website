Requirements

Owner-Operator Minimum Requirements

We run a tight, DOT-compliant program. Review every category below before you apply — MayoMile evaluates qualification on a case-by-case basis.

Age & License

  • At least 23 years of age
  • Class A CDL — or Class B CDL for Expedited operations — with HazMat (H) or combination (X) endorsement

Driving Record (previous 36 months)

  • No more than two at-fault accidents and two moving violations — or no more than one at-fault accident and three moving violations
  • No preventable DOT-recordable accident within the past 12 months
  • No license suspension exceeding 30 days for moving violations
  • No more than one serious violation under FMCSR §383.51 Table 2
  • No railroad-highway grade-crossing offenses under FMCSR §383.51 Table 3
  • No citations or convictions for reckless driving or careless endangerment

Experience

  • At least 1 year verifiable OTR experience within the past 3 years, or 2 years within the past 5 years
  • Expedited operations: minimum 6 months verifiable OTR experience may be accepted
  • Experience must be on equipment comparable to what you'll operate with MayoMile

Background & Legal

  • No felony convictions within the past 7 years (other charges reviewed case-by-case)
  • No positive drug or alcohol tests — SAP program completion does not waive this requirement
  • No DUI charges within the past 60 months in a personal or non-commercial vehicle; CMV DUI charges or convictions are never permitted
  • Sufficient English proficiency to read signage, respond to official inquiries, complete reports, and communicate with the public

These are minimum owner-operator qualification standards and remain subject to all applicable DOT requirements. MayoMile may impose additional or more stringent requirements at its discretion. Qualification decisions are made by MayoMile on a case-by-case basis.
